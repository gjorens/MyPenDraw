Dimensions

Dimensions show the width and height of a rectangle or oval as you draw it. Keep them to leave them on the drawing.

Drag to size a shape. An arc runs from where the pen goes down to where it lifts, and bulges toward the side you first moved.

Grid

Units
Spacing
1/2 in
Snapping

Spacing uses 96 drawing units per inch. Dimensions and measurements use the same unit. Screen scale varies between iPad models, so a printed inch may not match exactly.

Eraser

Width 24

Drag over ink to erase it. The eraser cuts through strokes and shapes on the active layer.

Files

Add a picture

Supported: SVG, PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF (first frame only). Up to 10 MB per file. Pictures go on the active layer, and you can then move, resize and rotate them.

SVG files stay sharp when enlarged. Once placed, an imported picture is a single object. Its parts can't be selected separately.

Project

A project keeps every layer, object, text and picture, so you can reopen it later or on another device. Saving opens the share sheet, where you can choose Save to Files or iCloud Drive.
