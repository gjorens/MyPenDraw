Dimensions

Dimensions show the width and height of a rectangle or oval as you draw it. For an arc, the width is the chord and the height is the bulge. Keep them to leave them on the drawing.

Drag to size a shape. An arc runs from where the pen goes down to where it lifts, and bulges toward the side you first moved. Select an arc, then drag its diamond handle to change the bulge. Drag past the chord to flip the arc to the other side.

Files

Add a picture

Supported: SVG, PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF (first frame only). Up to 10 MB per file. Pictures go on the active layer, and you can then move, resize and rotate them.

SVG files stay sharp when enlarged. Once placed, an imported picture is a single object. Its parts can't be selected separately.

Project
Print

Makes a one-page PDF at the drawing's real size, from the top-left of the screen. Only visible layers are included, and the grid is included when its layer is shown. Scale it to 100% when printing.

A project keeps every layer, object, text and picture, so you can reopen it later or on another device. Saving opens the share sheet, where you can choose Save to Files or iCloud Drive.
